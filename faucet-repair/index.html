Emergency Faucet Repair in Manchester Township, NJ

Faucet repair replaces the worn cartridge, valve seat, O-ring or supply line behind a drip or leak, or swaps the whole fixture when it is beyond saving.

Call (609) 547-1417

Signs a faucet is failing

A faucet rarely fails all at once. Most give a warning for weeks, and a small drip wastes more water than people think. Here is what the usual symptoms mean.

  • Steady drip from the spoutA worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Left alone, the seat can pit and make the repair larger.
  • Leak at the handleA failing O-ring or packing nut. Water runs down the body and can reach the cabinet below.
  • Water under the sinkA cracked supply line, loose coupling or a leaking angle stop, not always the faucet.
  • Low or uneven flowMineral debris or iron sediment clogging the aerator or cartridge.
  • Handle will not hold temperatureA worn mixing cartridge in a single-handle faucet.
  • Tub spout or shower faucet dripsA worn cartridge or diverter. A shower that keeps running wastes hot water all day.

Water spraying under the sink or a valve that will not close

A burst supply line or a faucet that will not shut off is a real emergency in a kitchen or bathroom. Look under the sink for two small oval or round valves, called angle stops. Turn each clockwise to close it, which stops water to the faucet without shutting off the house.

If the angle stop is seized, leaking or missing, shut the main water valve where the line enters the house and call (609) 547-1417. Dispatch answers 24/7 and books same-day when available. Do not use pliers to force an old stop, because a snapped stem makes the leak worse.

Before you go to bed

If a supply line is weeping under a cabinet, put a pan beneath it and check it in an hour. A steady drip that keeps growing needs a call tonight, not tomorrow.

Cartridge, ball, disc or compression: what is inside

The repair depends on the faucet's inner design. Knowing which type you have helps a plumber bring the right part. The table covers the common ones.

Faucet types and typical repairs
TypeHow it failsRepair
CartridgeDrip or hard handleReplace the cartridge and O-rings
BallLeak at the base of the handleReplace the springs, seats and ball
Ceramic discDrip at the spout, rareReplace the disc cylinder
CompressionDrip when the handle is offReplace washers and seats
Pull-down kitchenHose leak or weak sprayReplace hose or the whole faucet

Parts for older faucets can be discontinued, which is when a replacement faucet makes more sense.

A plumber who sees your faucet can usually tell by the handle and the base. If the model is old and parts are hard to find, replacing the whole faucet often costs about what a rare part would.

Repair or replace the faucet

Repair makes sense when the finish is good and the model is still sold. Replace when the body is corroded, the finish is flaking, or the repair is likely to fail again. Replacement is also a chance to upgrade to a pull-down spray or a lever handle that is easier on arthritic hands.

  • Repair: drips, loose handles and a faucet in good shape
  • Replace: corroded bodies, flaking finish, or discontinued parts
  • Replace: when you want a new style or a lever handle for easier use

Faucets in Manchester Township homes

The township's median home was built in 1979, and over half of the housing stock predates 1980. That means many kitchens and baths still have faucets and angle stops that are decades old. When we replace a faucet, we usually swap the stops and supply lines too, so the next repair is easier. A bathroom visit often pairs with toilet repair, and a kitchen visit with disposal service.

Local water is moderately soft to soft, so scale is a smaller problem than in many parts of New Jersey. In parts of the western system near Whiting, iron above the utility's secondary guideline can stain sinks and clog aerators with orange grit. If your aerator keeps blocking, the cartridge is worth checking at the same visit. We also serve Toms River and Lakewood.

Residents in the active-adult communities often ask for lever handles, touch-on faucets or taller spouts that clear a large pot. Those are simple upgrades when the faucet is being replaced anyway.

What changes the cost of a faucet repair

  • Part typeA cartridge or washer is inexpensive. A designer or discontinued part costs more.
  • Repair or full replacementA new faucet adds the fixture and the time to fit it.
  • Seized or leaking angle stopsA new stop and supply line add a small part cost but prevent the next emergency.
  • Access under the sinkTight cabinets and old plumbing take longer to reach.
  • After-hours callLate-night emergency calls are priced differently from daytime visits. You hear the price first.
