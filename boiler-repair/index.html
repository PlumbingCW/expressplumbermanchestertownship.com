Boiler Repair in Manchester Township, NJ

Boiler repair means diagnosing why a hydronic heating boiler leaks, short-cycles or stops making heat, then fixing the failed part. Call when the house is cold.

Call (609) 547-1417

No heat in cold weather: what to check first

A boiler that stops in January is a real problem, especially with older family members at home. Before you call, run through a short checklist. Many service calls start with a simple cause.

  1. Make sure the thermostat is set to heat and above room temperature.
  2. Check that the boiler power switch and breaker are on.
  3. Look at the pressure gauge. Low pressure often points to a leak or a failed fill valve.
  4. Check the oil tank level or confirm that gas service is on.
  5. Look for water around the boiler or on nearby floors.

Gas or oil smells

If you smell gas, leave the home and call the gas utility or 911 first. For a smoke or fuel odor, ventilate and stop using the boiler until it has been checked.

Common boiler problems

  • Short cyclingThe boiler turns on and off in quick bursts. Causes include an oversized unit, bad controls, low water or a clogged heat exchanger.
  • Banging or kettlingMineral buildup or trapped air makes the water boil in pockets.
  • Leaks at the boiler or pipesPressure relief valve, circulator flange or a failing heat exchanger.
  • Radiators cold at the topTrapped air needs bleeding. If it keeps coming back, a leak is adding air.
  • Pilot or burner will not lightIgnition parts, a dirty burner or a gas valve.
  • Rising or dropping pressureA failed expansion tank or a failed fill valve.

Standards behind heating boilers

Residential heating boilers are built to the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section IV, and boiler replacements in New Jersey fall under New Jersey's Uniform Construction Code plumbing subcode (N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15) along with other trades' rules. Older homes and boiler piping

About half of Manchester Township homes were built before 1980, so some heating loops and valves are decades old. Old zone valves and circulators fail more often, and old fittings can leak when the system is cycled hard in the first cold snap.

If your boiler also supplies domestic hot water, a failure can leave you without heat and hot water at once. Tell dispatch so the visit covers both. Water here is soft to moderately soft, at about 3 to 6 grains per gallon, so scale in the loop is mild, but iron in parts of the western system can still settle in small passages.

Factors that change a boiler repair price

  • Part that failedThermostats and circulators cost less than gas valves or heat exchangers.
  • Fuel typeOil and gas boilers have different parts and tests.
  • Age and parts supplyOlder boilers can need hard-to-find parts.
  • AccessBasement stairs, tight spaces and long runs add time.
  • Time of dayA cold-night emergency visit can cost more than a daytime booking, and you hear the price first.
