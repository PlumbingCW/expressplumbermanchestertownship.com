Before the plumber arrives: protect the house

Shut the main

Close the main water valve. Homes on a private well should switch off the well pump at its breaker after the valve is closed.

Kill power near water

If water reaches outlets, turn off that circuit only when the panel is dry and easy to reach.

Lift and move

Raise furniture, boxes and electronics off wet floors, especially in low areas near the bay.

Call and describe

Call (609) 547-1417, give your cross street, and tell dispatch whether you have public sewer or septic.

Gas smell

If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility from outside. A gas leak is not a plumbing emergency.

Public water, private wells and sewer pump stations

The Berkeley Township Municipal Utilities Authority (PWSID NJ1505004) supplies public water. A state infrastructure bank notice says its service area does not cover the whole township, and some residents rely on shallow private wells. If you are on a well, tell dispatch, because low pressure may start outside the plumbing inside your house.

The township master plan lists extending public water and sewer to existing neighborhoods as a goal. That means some homes are not yet on public sewer. Before you assume a sewer line problem, find out whether your home is on public sewer or a septic system.

The Berkeley Township Sewerage Authority runs fourteen sanitary sewer pump stations, according to the state's municipal report. When storms knock a station offline, backups can show up in low areas at once. If your neighbors have the same problem, report it to the authority and call us for anything inside your own walls.

Retirement communities, Bayville and Pine Beach

Berkeley Township has many adult communities, including Holiday City at Berkeley, Holiday City South, Holiday City West, Holiday Heights, Silver Ridge Park and Sonata Bay. The state report places Holiday City and Silver Ridge west of the Garden State Parkway, inside the Pinelands area. Check association rules before major work, since pipe changes may need approval.

The 2020-2024 Census data shows 21,950 households averaging 2.01 people, and 88.9 percent are owner-occupied. Owners decide their own repairs, so we explain the options and the price first. If a water heater fails, the owner makes the call, so we lay out repair and replacement choices first.

Bayville is where the township mailing address sits, with the municipal complex on Pinewald-Keswick Road. Pine Beach is a separate borough next door, and our line takes calls from there too. Your ZIP does not change how we answer.

Why the bay shapes plumbing here

The state report says much of the township lies in flood-prone areas, with about 35 miles of shoreline. Superstorm Sandy left 252 properties substantially damaged and seven sewage pump stations offline. Homes near the water should keep drains and floor drains clear before storms.

After flooding, standing water can leave sediment in pipes and damage the heater or pump. A leak detection visit can find a hidden break before it ruins floors. Low spots near the bay may also need a sump pump repair before the next storm.

Nearly 47 percent of the township is public land, much of it Pinelands preserve. Public land covers so much ground that street names repeat across areas, so give your cross street and the nearest landmark when you call.
