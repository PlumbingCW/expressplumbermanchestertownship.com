Single-family houses in a 55 and older community

Community listings describe Leisure Knoll as houses for residents 55 and older, with building from 1973 to 1999 and about 1,626 homes. The 2020 Census counted 2,562 residents. Houses differ from the attached homes nearby, because the lines and fixtures are usually yours alone.

Where the township places Leisure Knoll

The township utility lists Leisure Knoll in the Eastern Service Area. Its 2025 data showed hardness of about 3 to 5 grains per gallon and iron from not detected up to 0.15 parts per million. Water is moderately soft, and iron staining is not a common worry.

A house built in the 1970s or 1980s may have its original shutoff valves and some older fixtures. Operate each shutoff once a year so it does not seize. A valve that will not turn is better found on a calm day than during a leak.

Water heaters and long-lived tanks

Many Leisure Knoll homes have been owned for a long time, so tanks tend to outlast a single owner's memory of when they were installed. The label on the side shows the manufacture date in the serial number. If you cannot read it, tell dispatch the make and the tank size.

Signs a tank is near the end include rust-colored hot water, a puddle at the base and a rumbling sound. A leak from the tank body cannot be repaired. See water heater replacement and installation for what to expect.
