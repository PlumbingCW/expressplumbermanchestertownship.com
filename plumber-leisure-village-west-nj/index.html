Attached homes change the plumbing question

Community listings describe Leisure Village West as a 55 and older community of attached homes. The 2020 Census counted 3,692 residents. The listings say building ran from 1972 to 1993 and that the community is gated.

In attached homes, a leak or a drain backup can cross a wall. If water appears on a shared wall or in a neighbor's unit, tell the association as well as a plumber. Our crew handles problems inside your own home, and the association usually handles shared building lines.

Before calling, shut off the water to the fixture if you can. If it is not clear where the water is coming from, close the main shutoff and call.

Gates, access and parking

If the entrance is gated, tell dispatch the gate procedure when you call. Give your court or lane, the building letter and any gate code process. That information keeps a technician from waiting at the entrance.

Parking can be limited, so mention where a work vehicle can stop. A short note about access saves time on a visit.

Water from the Eastern system

The township utility lists Leisure Village West in its Eastern Service Area. The 2025 data for that system showed hardness of about 3 to 5 grains per gallon and iron from not detected to 0.15 parts per million. That is moderately soft water with low iron.

Faucet aerators and showerheads can still collect mineral buildup over time. If flow has dropped at one fixture, the aerator may be clogged. If it has dropped everywhere, tell dispatch, because the cause may be pressure or a valve.

Everyday fixes in a village home

  1. Check the fixture shutoffTurn the small valve under the sink or behind the toilet. If it will not turn, mention that on the call.
  2. Note when it happensSay whether the problem is hot only, cold only or both. It narrows the cause quickly.
  3. Ask about shared partsIf a repair involves a wall, riser or outside spigot, check with the association first.
  4. Call for the restFor faucets, toilets, drains and water heaters inside your unit, call us.
