Inside Manchester Township

Manchester Township is in Ocean County, with a population of about 46,556. Whiting is the largest community, and County Route 530 is the main road through it. Route 70 crosses the township, and Route 37 runs through the eastern part. County routes 539, 547 and 571 also serve the area, along with local roads like Lacey Road and Lakewood Avenue.

Within the township, calls come from Whiting, Crestwood Village, Pine Ridge at Crestwood, Leisure Village West, Leisure Knoll, Cedar Glen Lakes, Cedar Glen West and Pine Lake Park. These are neighborhoods and communities, not separate towns, so they all share one dispatch line and one set of plumbing services.

The age of the housing matters here. The median home was built in 1979 and about 52 percent were built before 1980. In the active-adult communities, homes built in the same era share aging parts, so water heater, valve and drain calls often cluster in the same streets.

Two water systems, one township

The Manchester Township Water Utility runs two systems. The Eastern Service Area covers portions along Route 37 and east of Route 70, and it also serves Lakehurst Borough. Its 2025 hardness ran about 3 to 5 grains per gallon, with iron mostly near or below detection.

The Western Service Area covers the Whiting section southeast of Manchester Boulevard, northwest of Lake Road and along Station Road. Hardness there was about 4 to 6 grains per gallon, and iron reached above the 0.3 ppm staining guideline in some samples. So orange stains on tubs and laundry are a real possibility on that side.

Practical result: water heaters in both areas gather sediment, so a yearly flush is worth doing. If you are on a private well instead, mention that when you call, because filters, iron and pump issues change the plan. Not every home is on public sewer either, so tell dispatch if you have a septic system.

Nearby towns we answer for

Each page below covers how plumbing calls work in that town, from housing age to water supply.

Jackson

Jackson is a large township in northwest Ocean County with about 58,500 residents in 2020. The township covers about 100 square miles, so a Jackson address can be far from a major road. Tell dispatch your cross streets, and say whether you are on public water and sewer or on a well and septic.

Toms River

Toms River is the Ocean County seat and a busy area with older homes. Its median year built is 1978, and about 54 percent of homes predate 1980. Expect the same kinds of calls we see in Manchester: tired water heaters, aging drain lines and corroded shutoffs.

Lakehurst

Lakehurst is a small borough of under a square mile, wedged next to Manchester Township. Its ZIP, 08733, reaches into land outside the borough, so give your street address, not just the ZIP. Because it is so close, many Lakehurst calls are straightforward for dispatch.

Lakewood

Lakewood is dense and busy, with more than 135,000 residents in 2020 and a large rental share. For multi-unit buildings, tell dispatch the unit number and who approves work. That keeps access and billing simple.

Berkeley Township

Berkeley Township sits partly inside the Pinelands, with nearly half its land in public hands. Not all of it is on the municipal water system, so some homes rely on shallow private wells. Say whether you are on a well, because it affects pumps, filters and what can go wrong.

Outer areas by request

We also take calls from places like Bayville, Pine Beach, Barnegat, Brick, Forked River and Lanoka Harbor, and from Pemberton Township and Browns Mills in the outer zone. These are outside our main pages, but dispatch will tell you whether a plumber can cover your address.
