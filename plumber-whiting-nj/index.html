Whiting is a mailing address, not one kind of home

Whiting is the largest part of Manchester Township. The 2020 Census counted 33,180 of the township's 45,115 residents here. That is a lot of houses, and they are not alike.

The Census places several named communities inside Whiting, including Crestwood Village, Cedar Glen Lakes, Cedar Glen West and Pine Ridge at Crestwood. Many addresses in 08759 are in age-restricted villages with their own offices. Others are on regular streets where the homeowner handles everything.

That split matters on a plumbing call. A homeowner on a regular street can call us directly. A resident of a village should first ask the village office what the maintenance fee covers, because some repairs may already be the association's job. See Crestwood Village and Cedar Glen Lakes for those communities.

Not sure whose repair it is? Call and describe what you see, and dispatch will help you sort it out.

Which water system your Whiting address uses

Manchester Township runs two water systems, split at the Route 70 Lakehurst Circle. The township's utility page puts Crestwood Villages 1 through 7, Pine Ridge, Pine Ridge South, Meadows and Whiting Station in the Western Service Area. Cedar Glen Lakes is listed there for sewer only.

The Western system reported iron up to 1.08 parts per million in 2025 testing, above the 0.3 guideline for staining. That is a range for the whole system, not a reading for your street. If you see orange rings in a toilet or rust on laundry, tell dispatch, because the cause might be iron in the water and not a failing pipe.

Hardness in the Western system ran about 4 to 6 grains per gallon in the 2025 data, which is moderately soft. Scale is mild, but tank water heaters still collect sediment as years pass. Check your bill or call the utility at the township to confirm which system serves your house.

Roads and landmarks we use to find you

Whiting is spread out, so directions matter. County Route 530 is the main road through Whiting, and Lakewood Avenue is a local road there. Manchester Boulevard, Lake Road and Station Road appear in the utility's description of the western system.

When you call, give the nearest cross street and the village or section name. A street name alone can repeat across several villages, and the village name is what gets a technician to the right gate or office.

Older housing and what it means for a call

Across the township, the median home was built in 1979, and about 52 percent of homes date from before 1980. Many Whiting homes come from the 1970s boom, and water heaters, shutoff valves and fixtures from that era may be on their second or third replacement.

Age alone does not mean a problem. It means checking the basics, such as the shutoff valves under sinks and the age stamped on the water heater label. If a valve will not turn or a tank is more than ten years old, mention it when you call.
