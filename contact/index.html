Emergency or routine?

Pick the right kind of call
SituationWhat to do
Sewage backing up, burst pipe, ceiling leakCall now, any hour. Shut off water first if you can
No hot water, sump pump failed in a stormCall now. Same-day appointments are offered when available
Dripping faucet, slow drain, running toiletCall during the day to book a time
Smell of gasLeave, call 911 or the gas utility, then call us later

Not sure which row you are in? Call and ask.

What to have ready when you call

Your address

Street, town and any nearby crossroad. For a rental, include the unit number.

What you see

Where the water is, what it looks like and what sounds or smells came first.

What you did

Say whether you shut the main valve or tried a plunger so dispatch can skip steps.

Access details

Gate codes, pets, who will be home and where the main shutoff is.

Water and sewer type

Public water or private well, public sewer or septic, if you know.

What happens after you call

  1. Dispatch confirmsThey repeat the address and problem, then give shutoff or safety steps.
  2. You hear the priceA clear price is given before work starts.
  3. A plumber is setEmergencies go first. Other work gets same-day appointments when available.
  4. Work and wrap-upThe plumber fixes the issue and explains what was found.

Why there is no form

A web form waits for someone to read it. A plumbing problem does not wait. By taking calls, we can ask about the water, the valve and the floor, and give you steps right away.
