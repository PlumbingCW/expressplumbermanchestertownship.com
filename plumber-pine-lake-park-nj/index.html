Plumber in Pine Lake Park, Manchester Township, NJ

Pine Lake Park is the township's largest development, built around a man-made lake. Plumbing calls here look like those in any busy family neighborhood.

A lake neighborhood with busy households

Pine Lake Park is the largest development in Manchester Township. Its man-made Pine Lake was built in the 1970s. The 2020 Census counted 8,913 people in 3,029 households, and 35.4 percent of those households had children under 18.

That mix matters for plumbing because households with children use more hot water, run more laundry and flush more. A water heater that was sized for fewer people may struggle. If your hot water runs out during back-to-back showers, say so on the call.

Hot water running out early or a drain backing up? Call and tell dispatch which fixture is affected.

Where Pine Lake Park sits in the township system

The township's utility page puts Pine Lake Park in the Eastern Service Area, east of the Route 70 Lakehurst Circle. The Eastern system's 2025 data showed hardness of about 3 to 5 grains per gallon and iron from not detected up to 0.15 parts per million.

That is moderately soft water with very little iron. Heavy limescale is unlikely, so a tank heater's main enemy is sediment that settles over time. Flushing a tank each year is a practical habit, and a loud popping or rumbling sound is a sign sediment has built up.

Housing from the lake-development era

The Census counted 3,189 housing units in Pine Lake Park in 2020, with 5.0 percent vacant. Across the township the median home was built in 1979, and about 52 percent date from before 1980. Pine Lake Park fits that 1970s window, though we do not have a build-year figure for the neighborhood itself.

What that means for you is simple. Check the age label on your water heater, test the shutoff valves under sinks and toilets, and look for green crust or white mineral rings on visible fittings. Small checks now keep a minor leak from becoming a stained ceiling.

Homes near the lake or low spots can see damp basements or crawl spaces after heavy rain. That is a drainage question first, and a sump pump question if you already have a pit.

Common calls from the Pine Lake area

  • Water heater replacement when a tank is old or leaking
  • Drain cleaning for kitchen and bath lines
  • Garbage disposal repair when a unit jams or hums
  • Toilet repair for constant running or weak flushes

Some homes in the township are on septic and some are on public sewer. Ask which you have before assuming a sewer problem. For nearby coverage see the Lakehurst page.
